Before you ask

Will a bot change my client's budget?

No. Bots recommend. By design, they cannot send a budget or setting change. A person approves, declines, or modifies it, and every decision is designed to be logged.

Do my clients see what we spend?

That is your call. Client views are designed to hide spend when needed.

Where do the audiences come from?

The live data market, not the ad platforms. jAI is built to work from mobile ad IDs (MAIDs) acquired from vetted vendors, uploaded to each channel where its audience policies allow, with pixels to serve and retarget.
